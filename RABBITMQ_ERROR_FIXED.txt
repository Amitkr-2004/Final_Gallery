================================================================================
  ❓ FAQ
================================================================================

Q: Why did this happen?
A: The default Celery configuration uses RabbitMQ, which wasn't installed.

Q: Is filesystem broker production-ready?
A: No, for production use RabbitMQ or Redis. But perfect for development!

Q: Do I need to install anything else?
A: No! The filesystem broker works out of the box.

Q: Will my old jobs still work?
A: Yes! All existing jobs will work fine.

Q: Can I switch back to RabbitMQ later?
A: Yes, just install RabbitMQ and change CELERY_BROKER_URL in settings.py
