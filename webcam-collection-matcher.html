📖 How to Use:

  1. Make sure your Electron app is running (the main gallery app)
  2. Open DevTools in the Electron app (F12)
  3. Keep DevTools open in the background
  4. Come back to this page and click "Start Camera"
  5. Click "Scan & Match" to find your collections

💡 Note: This page uses the Electron app's APIs to search collections. The simple API server doesn't have database access for collection matching.
